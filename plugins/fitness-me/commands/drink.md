Log a drink with the Fitness Me `fm_command` tool: `{"command":"drink","args":["$ARGUMENTS"]}`, splitting what I wrote into separate args — e.g. "monster" → `{"command":"drink","args":["monster"]}`, "2 kaffe" → `{"command":"drink","args":["kaffe","2"]}`, "cola zero 50cl" → `{"command":"drink","args":["cola-zero","50cl"]}`, "Monster Ultra" → `{"command":"drink","args":["energy","--sugar-free","--name","Monster Ultra"]}`. A bare count is servings of that drink (energy drink 500 ml, soda 330 ml, coffee 250 ml, beer 330 ml); a volume is logged as is. If I give label numbers, pass them as whole-drink totals (`--kcal`, `--sugar`, `--caffeine`). "again" or "samme igen" → `{"command":"drink","args":["again"]}`. With nothing given, show `{"command":"drink","args":["recent"]}` and ask which one. Then tell me in one line what was logged and, from `day`, my water progress, caffeine against the limit (e.g. "Koffein 300 / 400 mg") and kcal from drinks — noting that alcohol doesn't count toward water.
